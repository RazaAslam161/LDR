## A. Why the app is laggy — ranked

**Lead finding:** the animated `EmberBackground` is the prime suspect for the "laggy everywhere, even on screens with no images" reports. It is the only cost that is present on *every* screen at *every* moment: one full-screen `CustomPaint` repainting on every vsync for the life of the process, plus a second identical instance on 12 screens (grep-confirmed: 12 `body: EmberBackground(` wrappers + the root at `main.dart:392`). Because it is the layer every `BackdropFilter` samples, it also prevents any glass panel in the app from ever being cached — the blurs re-composite because the backdrop under them is dirty every frame. Everything else on this list is a screen-local cost; this one is global. **How much of the problem it is: unmeasured.** No profiling was run. Treat it as the first thing to instrument, not as a proven number.

## B. Fix plan by risk tier

### Tier 0 — apply now (zero risk, mechanical, no behaviour change)

1. **Stop awaiting AdService** — `mobile/lib/main.dart:72` → delete the await, call `unawaited(AdService.init())` after `runApp`. Nothing reads `AdService` state (grep: the class and `main.dart:72` are the only hits) and per `google_mobile_ads 9.0.0` the first ad request self-initializes the SDK. *(File is `lib/core/ads/ad_service.dart`, not `lib/core/ad_service.dart`.)*
2. **Stop awaiting FcmService** — `mobile/lib/main.dart:73` → `unawaited(FcmService.init())`. Eight serial platform crossings for `pendingReach`/`pendingCall` notifiers that `AppShell` explicitly drains in its post-frame callback (`app_shell.dart:112-113`). Leave `onBackgroundMessage` at `:65` where it is.
3. **Hoist the per-frame `Paint` allocations in `_EmberPainter`** — `ember_background.dart:143-165`: give `_Star` a `final Paint` and mutate `.color`; reuse one `Paint` for all 14 embers (the `MaskFilter` is already `const`). Removes ~48 allocations per frame per instance with no pixel change.
4. **Index messages by id once per build** — `chat_screen.dart:261-267,920`: replace the `_byId` linear scan with a map built alongside the existing `_ids` set.
5. **Move the GIF image into `child:`** — `chat_screen.dart:1305-1346`: the `Image.network` belongs in the `AnimatedBuilder`'s `child` parameter, not rebuilt every frame for 5.2s.
6. **Timeline: load from `initState`, not `didChangeDependencies`** — `timeline_screen.dart:22-25`. Today a keyboard show/hide or rotation refetches the list and flashes a spinner.
7. **Guard the Home GPS `setState`** — `home_screen.dart:92`: add `&& (pos.latitude != _myLat || pos.longitude != _myLon)`. A stationary phone returns the identical fix every 15s. **Do not** move the coords to a `ValueNotifier` watched only by the distance row — they also feed the map polyline (`partner_location_card.dart:250-261`) and the "you are here" marker (`:272-278`), which would then render from a stale first build.
8. **`RepaintBoundary` around the root Stack's animated siblings** — `main.dart:388-418`: wrap `CallPill`, the `PartnerHereBadge` `Align`, and the `AppLock` `ValueListenableBuilder`. Zero visual change. (Honest caveat: this does **not** help the glass panels — a `BackdropFilter` samples through boundaries.)
9. **Type `_PartnerStatusCard.partner` as `Profile`** — `home_screen.dart:223`: it is currently `dynamic`, which forces dynamic dispatch and blocks const-construction on a hot path.
10. **Delete the unused `_ago` helper** — `chat_screen.dart:1035`.

### Tier 1 — low risk (needs review, no visible change)

1. **Add decode-size hints to every list/bubble image** — `chat_screen.dart:1420,1425,1341,1540`, `giphy_picker.dart:155`, `touch_map_screen.dart:1182,1635`, `capsule_detail_screen.dart:585`, `call_screen.dart:208`; and set `memCacheWidth`/`memCacheHeight` inside `net_image.dart:25` so all avatars downscale by default. Compute as `(layoutWidth * MediaQuery.devicePixelRatioOf(context)).round()`. **Review each site individually — `media_viewer.dart:30` is a full-screen viewer and must be capped at screen width, not bubble width.**
2. **Parallelise `main()`'s independent inits** — `main.dart:50-73`. Keep the env fail-fast at `:53-60` *between* `dotenv.load()` and the wait, then `await Future.wait([Firebase.initializeApp(...), SupabaseService.init(), MilesApp.loadSetupFlag()])`. `onBackgroundMessage`, `initRealtimeAutoResume()` and `FcmService.init()` all stay after the wait. **Keep `TzHelper.ensureInit()` at `:66`** — see Tier 2.
3. **Stop the presence realtime refetch** — `presence_service.dart:418-421` does a full network SELECT on every presence row change; the payload already carries the new row. Use it, or debounce. This is the real waste, and it is battery/network, not frames.
4. **Gate the presence poll on lifecycle** — `presence_service.dart:390`: add `WidgetsBindingObserver`, cancel on `paused`/`hidden`, restart on `resumed`. It is the only periodic in the app that runs while backgrounded; the pattern already exists at `home_screen.dart:57-65`.
5. **Stop the heartbeat camera + torch on background** — `heartbeat_screen.dart:85-97`: add `WidgetsBindingObserver` and call `_stopCamera()` on `paused`/`hidden`/`detached`, copying `touch_map_screen.dart:1895-1903`.
6. **Confine Touch Map's 18Hz tick to the neon painters** — `touch_map_screen.dart:677-686`: keep the 55ms `Timer` but have it bump a `ValueNotifier<int>`; wrap only `Positioned.fill` at `:1190-1199` in `RepaintBoundary` + `ValueListenableBuilder`, and move the `.where(...).toList()` filter inside that builder. **Do not** use a `ValueNotifier<List<_NP>>` — the list is mutated in place (`_neon.removeWhere`, `:680`), so it would not notify and trails would freeze mid-fade. Keep `shouldRepaint => true` (a fresh `DateTime.now()` per build makes it correct) and leave `_heatTimer` alone.
7. **`ListView.builder` for the unbounded lists** — `timeline_screen.dart:120` and `vault_screen.dart:139`, both currently eager `children:` over every row; also drop the per-row `IntrinsicHeight` at `timeline_screen.dart:300`.
8. **Drop `workmanager`** — `pubspec.yaml:81`. Zero imports in `lib/` or `test/`, but it drags `androidx.work` into the manifest merge and `androidx.startup` initialises it at launch. Rebuild and verify the app boots.
9. **Bundle Fraunces + Inter** — add them under `flutter.fonts` in `pubspec.yaml` and set `GoogleFonts.config.allowRuntimeFetching = false` so a missing asset fails loudly in dev.
10. **ABI split** — `android/app/build.gradle.kts`: add `splits { abi { isEnable = true; reset(); include("arm64-v8a", "armeabi-v7a"); isUniversalApk = false } }`. The three target phones (IN2015, Vivo, OnePlus 7) are all arm64. The audit measured x86_64 at 54.0 MB of the 159.9 MB APK by reading the shipped artifact; **re-measure after the split rather than quoting that figure forward.** Install size only — this will not change frame time.

### Tier 2 — visible trade-off (owner's decision)

1. **Remove the 12 per-screen `EmberBackground` wrappers**, keeping only `main.dart:392`. *Trade-off:* this is **not** behaviour-identical. `_EmberPainter` is entirely size-relative — the glow centre, the falloff radius, and all 34 star / 14 ember positions are computed from the paint rect. The inner instance paints into the Scaffold body; the outer paints full-screen. On the 8 screens with an app bar (none sets `extendBodyBehindAppBar`) the field visibly rescales and shifts. It also *removes* the existing seam where the app-bar strip's field meets the body's field, and removes the phase drift between the two controllers — arguably an improvement, but it is a deliberate visual normalisation. **Apply screen by screen with before/after screenshots.**
2. **Add a `MilesPerf.lowGraphics` tier flag** resolved once at startup, and ship the blur bypass and the ember downgrade *in the same switch*. Apply the bypass to `GlassPanel`, `GlassNavBar`, `GlassPill` only — `GlassAppBar`, `GlassCircleButton` and `GlassTile` have **zero call sites** in `lib/` or `test/`. *Trade-offs:* (a) if the blur is removed without also baking `EmberBackground` to a static gradient, the 1–3px stars and embers read as sharp dots through `GlassPanel`'s 10%-white fill; (b) raising the fill to `glassStrong` to compensate makes every panel flatter and hazier on low tier; (c) **exclude** the `GlassPill` at `touch_map_screen.dart:2186` and the panels at `:1743`/`:2073` — they float over a live `CameraPreview` (`:2121`) and would show a sharp moving camera feed.
3. **Re-enable Impeller** — `AndroidManifest.xml:88` — and instead remove the `BackdropFilter`/blend-mode wrapper from the camera preview texture in `rapid_camera_screen.dart`, or gate that one screen. *Trade-off:* the flag exists because Impeller/Vulkan segfaulted on that composite. This must be re-tested on all three phones before it ships; a segfault is worse than jank. Risk: medium.
4. **Lower the theme sigmas** (`theme.dart:55-57`) and route `glass_panel.dart`'s six hardcoded literals through them. *Trade-off:* a design change. "Perceptually similar over a dark backdrop" is an unverified judgement — requires an A/B screenshot before committing.
5. **Switch to `package:timezone/data/latest_10y.dart`** — `mobile/lib/core/time/tz_helper.dart:1`. Measured: 445,672 B → 66,072 B of embedded data. *Trade-off:* the 10y DB drops deprecated zone **names**, not just history (e.g. `Asia/Calcutta`, `Europe/Kiev` are absent), and `_location` swallows a miss and silently returns UTC. **Before shipping, run `select distinct timezone from profiles;` and confirm every value is in `config.dart`'s `commonTimezones`** (all 17 of those are present in the 10y DB). **Do not delete `TzHelper.ensureInit()` at `main.dart:66`** — it is redundant, but removing it relocates a ~446KB synchronous parse out of startup and *into* the first build of `_PartnerStatusCard`/`_describeSky`, i.e. into a frame. Eager init before `runApp` is the correct placement.
6. **Pre-render the film grain** — `camera_filter_painter.dart:127`: draw a small tiling `ui.Image` with a per-frame offset instead of tens of thousands of `drawRect`s. *Trade-off:* a tiled grain can show visible repetition; needs a look-check against the current output.
7. **Unbundle ML Kit** — `pubspec.yaml:88`: switch pose detection and subject segmentation to the Play-services (downloaded-model) variants. *Trade-off:* Touch Map's gesture/segmentation features need network on first use. **Do not remove the feature** — it is drawer-reachable and intentional. Audit-measured: 28.4 MB of `.so` across ABIs + 12.3 MB of `assets/mlkit_pose`.
8. **Replace the ember `MaskFilter.blur` with a `drawAtlas` sprite** — *only after profiling confirms it matters.* If done, author the sprite with constant halo padding (the current `MaskFilter` uses a fixed sigma 2 regardless of the circle's 1.0–3.2 radius, so a naively scaled sprite loses the small embers' proportionally large glow), and use `colors` + `BlendMode.modulate` for the per-ember alpha fade. **Do not** bake the gradient or the starfield into a `ui.Picture` recorded once per size — that kills the 36s candle breath and the independent per-star twinkle.

**Explicitly rejected, do not put in the sprint:**
- *`TickerMode`/route-gating the `EmberBackground` controllers.* Flutter already mutes route-scoped tickers (`overlay.dart:907` + `ticker_provider.dart:389`), so the 11 in-route instances stop for free when covered. The one that never stops (`main.dart:392`) lives above the Navigator where route awareness cannot reach it — and stopping it would freeze the backdrop every glass panel blurs.
- *Adding `==`/`hashCode` to `Presence`.* Provable no-op: `StateNotifier.updateShouldNotify` is `!identical(old, current)`; Riverpod never calls `==` on this state. And gating the poll's emit on value equality breaks the feature the poll exists for — after a partner force-kills the app the row stops changing, so the UI would never flip Online → Offline.
- *Carving the AppBar and the bubble list out of Chat's 5s refresh.* `_ChatSubtitle` (`:1003,1016,1028`) and the seen tick (`:927-929` via `_statusFor`) are both wall-clock-derived, so the "only the seen tick and 'is here' are time-dependent" premise is false. `_messages` is capped at 300 (`chat_repository.dart:164`) and `ListView.builder` with `ValueKey`s updates elements in place, so the rebuild is small. The thing worth questioning here is the **5s network write** (`PresenceService.setChatLastRead`), not the rebuild.

## D. What cannot be known without a device

**No fps was measured. No frame times, no DevTools timeline, no `--profile` build, no `--trace-startup`.** Every performance claim above is inferred from reading source. The mechanisms are verified; the magnitudes are not, and no number in this document should be treated as a measurement unless it is explicitly labelled as one (only the APK byte sizes and the tzdata file sizes are).

Before landing anything in Tier 2, and before claiming any of this fixed, run on a real target phone (IN2015 / Vivo / OnePlus 7 — the mid/low-tier devices that generated the complaints):

1. **`flutter run --profile` + DevTools Performance timeline, idle on Home.** This is the single decisive measurement. It answers whether the steady-state cost lives on the **UI thread** (rebuilds — findings 5, 6, 10) or the **raster thread** (the ember painter and the blurs — findings 1, 4). The entire ranking above hinges on that split, and it is currently a guess.
2. **Repaint rainbow + "Track widget rebuilds"** on Home and Chat, to size how much finding 6 actually costs versus the always-dirty backdrop underneath it. If the backdrop is repainting every frame regardless, halving Home's rebuild cadence may show up as nothing.
3. **Whether `MaskFilter.blur` on 14 small circles is actually expensive on these GPUs.** The claim that it is "uncached, one GPU pass per circle" is an assertion, not a measurement. Adreno drivers have fast paths for blurred circles. Do not do the `drawAtlas` rewrite (Tier 2 item 8) until this is measured.
4. **Impeller before/after.** Two things need answering: the frame-time delta from re-enabling it, and whether the `rapid_camera_screen` Vulkan segfault still reproduces on Flutter 3.44 on all three phones. The second is a go/no-go gate, not a nice-to-have.
5. **Cold start.** `--trace-startup`, plus a `Stopwatch` around each await in `main()`, before and after the Tier 1 restructure. Right now nobody knows whether the pre-frame cost is dominated by Supabase session recovery, `MobileAds.initialize`, the ML Kit `ContentProvider` that runs before `Application.onCreate`, or the tzdata parse.
6. **Image memory.** DevTools memory view with "Track image allocations" while scrolling a media-heavy chat, to confirm the `ImageCache` is actually thrashing rather than just being large. Also re-measure **after** adding decode hints — do not tune `cacheExtent` before that, since a larger cache extent with undownscaled decodes makes things strictly worse.
7. **Screenshot diffs**, not opinions, for: the 12 ember-wrapper removals (each screen), the sigma retune, the low-tier no-blur look, the tiled grain, and the de-blurred chat save buttons. Every one of those is a visual change being justified on a perception argument that has not been checked.
8. **Re-measure the APK** after `--split-per-abi` rather than carrying the 90.7 MB / 159.9 MB figures forward as predictions.
