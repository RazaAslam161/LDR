Delete your Miles account

You can do this here, in a browser, without reinstalling the app. Read what it removes first — most of it cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in — you will not be able to log in again.
  • Your profile: name, time zone, date of birth, notification token, and the reference to your profile photo. The photo file itself lives in the couple’s shared media and goes when that does.
  • Your link to your partner. You are removed from the couple immediately.
  • Your personal vault, your cycle data, your saved encryption backup, and everything else recorded against your account alone. Your vault’s files are erased from storage by a job that runs every hour.
  • The diagnostic records tied to your account — your crash reports, and any rows left in the retired trace table — which are removed with it rather than left to expire.
  • If nobody else is left in your couple — your partner has already left or deleted their account, or you never paired — the couple’s whole shared history goes with yours: every message, photograph, video, voice note, document and Closer item. Those files are erased from storage by the same hourly job.

What is retained, and for how long

  • If your partner still has their account: the conversation you shared stays with them. It is their data as much as yours, so deleting your account does not erase their copy. If they later delete their own account, it goes at once; if they only leave the couple, it goes under the rule below.
  • A couple that was ended by leaving, rather than by deletion, is removed 30 days after either of you leaves — messages, Closer content and every other record of it, automatically. Re-pairing inside those 30 days cancels it. The stored media files are the one thing that sweep does not currently erase; deleting your account, rather than leaving, is what erases them.
  • Safety reports. A report you filed is deleted with your account. A report your partner filed about you is not: it is kept with the link to your account removed, because a record that disappears the moment its subject deletes an account is not a safety record at all.
  • We keep no backups of our own and cannot restore a deleted account from one. Our hosting provider may hold short-term disaster-recovery copies on its own schedule; on our plan we can neither read them nor restore from them.

End-to-end encrypted content cannot be recovered after this, by you or by us. Two things can open it: the key on your phone, and the sealed copy of that key we hold, which your password unwraps. Deleting the account destroys the sealed copy, so once your phone’s copy is gone there is no third one. If you want a copy of anything, take it from the app before you continue.

Delete it

We send a code to that address and delete nothing until you enter it. That is what stops anyone else deleting your account.

Prefer to do it in the app?

Settings → Account → Delete account. Same effect, no code needed, because you are already signed in.
