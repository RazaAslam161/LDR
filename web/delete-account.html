Delete your Miles account

You can do this here, in a browser, without reinstalling the app. Read what it removes first — most of it cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in — you will not be able to log in again.
  • Your profile: name, photo, time zone, date of birth, notification token.
  • Your link to your partner. You are removed from the couple immediately.
  • Your personal vault, your cycle data, and everything else recorded against your account alone.
  • If your partner has already left or deleted their account: the couple’s whole shared history goes with yours — every message, photograph, video, voice note, document and Closer item. The files themselves are erased from storage by a job that runs every hour.

What is retained, and for how long

  • If your partner still has their account: the conversation you shared stays with them. It is their data as much as yours, so deleting your account does not erase their copy. It is deleted under the rule below when they leave or delete in turn.
  • A couple nobody is left in is deleted 30 days later — messages, media and Closer content, automatically and completely.
  • Diagnostic traces already collected expire on their own: crash reports after 14 days, performance traces after 2 days. They contain no message content.
  • Backups held by our hosting provider roll off on their own schedule.

Content encrypted with a key only your phone held cannot be recovered after this, by you or by us. If you want a copy of anything, take it from the app before you continue.
