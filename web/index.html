Miles — Legal & Safety

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Miles is a private messaging app for two adults in a relationship. It is rated 18+, it pairs only by invitation, and it has no public surface — no directory, no discovery, nothing anyone posts is visible to anybody but their partner. These are the documents that govern it. They are written to be accurate rather than reassuring: where the app's ability to act is limited, they say so.

  • Privacy Policy

    Everything the app collects, what is encrypted and what is not, who it is sent to, and how long it is kept.

  • Terms of Use

    The agreement you accept when you use Miles, including what may not be sent and what happens to an account that sends it.

  • Child Safety Standards

    Standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation, how to report it, and who to contact.

  • Delete your account

    Delete an account and its data from here, without needing to open the app.

  • FAQ

    Plain answers about encryption, recovery, pairing, notifications and leaving.

  • Security & Vulnerability Disclosure

    How to report a security problem, what is in scope, and what we promise in return. For researchers rather than for users.
